# Chart Label

A small four-line table pinned to the chart, useful for screenshots and trade
journals.

| Line | Shows | Example |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Ticker and timeframe | `MNQZ2026 1m` |
| 2 | Day and date of the last bar, in the symbol's exchange timezone | `WED 09/30/26` |
| 3 | Trading mode: Live, Backtesting or Paper Trading | `Live` |
| 4 | Any text you type | `Trade Info Here` |

## Settings

| Setting | Default | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Line 3 | Live | Live, Backtesting or Paper Trading |
| Line 4 | Trade Info Here | Free text |
| Position | Lower Center | Upper or Lower; Left, Center or Right |
| Size | Medium | Small, Medium or Large |
| Colors | White background; red line 1; black lines 2 and 4; blue line 3 | Each line has its own color |
| Show Border | on | Black, thickness 2 |
